# Research 014: polynomial synthesis
Use typed theorem corpora with provenance, licenses, assumptions and proof status. Sources: mathlib4, Isabelle AFP, Metamath set.mm, Rocq, Mizar, DLMF, arXiv (unverified claims separated). No corpus ingestion claimed.
For exponential moments P_k, a finite linear combination L=sum c_k P_k has Mellin multiplier Gamma(s) Q(s), Q(s)=sum c_k (s)_k, where (s)_k is rising factorial. The basis is triangular and monic, so every rational polynomial Q has a unique coefficient vector. Q(1)=0 cancels the main term; Q nonzero in 1/2<Re(s)<1 preserves hypothetical zero poles. For example Q=(s-1)^2 gives c=(1,-3,1). An unconditional square-root bound for L would imply RH; no such bound is established. General nonlinear polynomial candidates require separate proofs and typed domains. Never infer universal identities from finite samples.
